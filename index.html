Tiny and focused: write quick notes without leaving your browser. Everything is stored locally; no accounts, no analytics.

Install (developer mode)

1

Download or clone the repository to a folder on your machine.

2

Open Chrome (or Brave) and go to chrome://extensions/. Enable Developer mode.

3

Click Load unpacked and select the extension folder. The extension icon will appear in your toolbar.

4

To update: overwrite files in the folder and click Reload on the extension card in chrome://extensions/.

Auto-save behavior

The editor auto-saves after a brief pause. The footer displays "Auto-saved" when the content is persisted.

Privacy

No network requests, no analytics, no accounts. Data remains in your browser's local storage.
